## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, authoritative, and mission-critical visual foundation for enterprise-scale sovereign governance. Built for national land governance, administrative revenue officers, cadastral surveyors, and institutional verifiers, the system fuses the constitutional gravitas of the Republic of India with modern institutional gov-tech efficiency.

### Visual Philosophy
The aesthetic is **Corporate Modern with High-Density Civic Precision**. It eschews consumer minimalism, whimsical ornamentation, and delicate glass treatments in favor of structured structural density, crisp contrast, institutional predictability, and zero-latency legibility. Every layout feels immovable, audited, and definitive.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Sovereign Trust:** Absolute legal standing, administrative rigor, and tamper-evident permanence.
- **Scientific Clarity:** High-density data displays that allow revenue collectors, tehsildars, and GIS analysts to process complex plot vectors, deed records, and AI anomaly matrices without visual fatigue.
- **Accessible Decisiveness:** Clear status hierarchies and definitive confidence thresholds that eliminate bureaucratic ambiguity across desktop workstation monitors and field-deployed rugged tablets.

## Colors

The color architecture communicates sovereign authority, institutional stability, and rapid operational triage. The primary canvas is configured for daytime administrative performance with rich semantic distinction.

### Primary Spectrum: Sovereign Navy
- **Primary Deep Sovereign (`#0F294A`)**: Used for institutional app bars, formal navigation ribbons, high-ranking executive containers, and legal deed headers.
- **Primary Navy Shade (`#1A365D`)**: Used for active side navigation, table header groupings, and primary action buttons.

### Functional Accents & Semantic Signals
- **Secondary Ashoka Blue (`#1D4ED8`)**: Represents cadastral layers, active record links, interactive data filters, and focus rings.
- **Tertiary Amber Gold (`#D97706`)**: Civic seal accents, pending validation alerts, cadastral boundary discrepancies, and medium-confidence AI flags. Darkened variant (`#B45309`) ensures WCAG AAA compliance on text labels.
- **Success Emerald (`#059669`)**: Clear mutation records, authenticated biometric signatures, and high-confidence AI parcel matching (>95%).
- **Critical Crimson (`#DC2626`)**: Contested title disputes, encroachment alerts, and validation failures.

### Canvas, Borders & Typography Neutrals
- **Surface Ground (`#F8FAFC`)**: High-contrast neutral background reducing eye strain over prolonged operational sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white card, drawer, and modal surfaces.
- **Border Structural (`#E2E8F0`)**: Crisp 1px division rules for high-density tables and layout blocks.
- **Border Accent (`#CBD5E1`)**: Used for input outlines and secondary segment controls.
- **Text Sovereign Dark (`#0F172A`)**: Ultra-high contrast (15.8:1) body copy and tabular readouts.
- **Text Slate Secondary (`#475569`)**: Subordinate metadata, survey numbers, and audit dates.
- **Text Slate Muted (`#64748B`)**: Field labels and inactive navigation anchors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes systematic clarity, bilingual parity, and optical performance in high-density enterprise tables.

### Font Hierarchy & Roles
- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers an authoritative, institutional geometry without feeling archaic. Used for module headers, section titles, parcel IDs, and major statistical summaries.
- **Body & Tabular (`Inter`)**: Chosen for its neutrality, comprehensive glyph support (vital for transliterated administrative vernacular), and tabular numeric features (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`). Used for land deeds, legal attestations, verification workflows, and raw data grids.

### Implementation Rules
- **Tabular Numerics**: All parcel areas (hectares/sq. meters), Khasra/Khatoni numbers, Aadhaar reference hashes, and confidence metrics must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain vertical alignment in lists and tables.
- **Label Capitalization**: `label-sm` and `label-md` elements utilize uppercase letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to ensure legibility when annotating status tags, classification codes, and district identifiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a high-density, fixed-fluid hybrid grid tailored for 24-inch government desktop workstations (1920x1080 standard) down to 10-inch ruggedized survey tablets.

### Layout Model
- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column structural grid on desktop with a fixed 260px administrative left rail for district/tehsil navigation.
- **Main Working Canvas**: Fluid container maximizing usable horizontal width with a max-width limit of `1680px` to maintain comfortable line lengths during long legal reading sessions.
- **Gutters & Margins**: Desktop uses `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` column gutters. In analytical split-views (Cadastral GIS map on the left, Digitized Deed on the right), gutters tighten to `0.75rem` (`gutter-compact`) to maximize real estate.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Behaviors
- **Desktop Large (≥1440px)**: Three-pane layout active (Hierarchy navigation + Cadastral Viewer/Grid + Inspection/Audit inspector).
- **Desktop Standard (1024px – 1439px)**: Two-pane layout with collapsible side inspector.
- **Tablet / Mobile (<1024px)**: Left rail collapses into an off-canvas drawer. Split views convert to full-width segmented tab views (e.g., Switch between "Map View", "Records Ledger", and "AI Validation").

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses **Tonal Layers with Low-Contrast Outlines** and purposeful, ultra-subtle ambient drop shadows. Heavy elevation, dramatic blur radii, and deep shadows are strictly avoided to ensure structural rigor and high visual density.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`)**: Level 0. Flat ground surface for the entire application interface.
- **Panel Surface (`#FFFFFF` + 1px `#E2E8F0` border)**: Level 1. Cards, table rows, and toolbars sit at this level. No shadow; separation is achieved entirely via the crisp `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Interactive Hover & Popover**: Level 2. `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.04)`. Outlines darken slightly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **System Modals & Critical Verification Drawers**: Level 3. `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.10), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.06)`. Border remains a razor-sharp 1px `#CBD5E1`.

### Data Hierarchy via Tonality
Hierarchy is established through surface color fills rather than elevation:
- System table headers use `#F1F5F9`.
- Selected table rows use an Ashoka Blue tint (`#EFF6FF`) with a solid 3px left border in `#1D4ED8`.
- Inactive panels receive a muted flat tone (`#F8FAFC`).

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. This balance provides a structured, professional enterprise demeanor without the harsh aggression of total 0px brutalism or the consumer feel of high-radius pill components.

### Radius Specifications
- **Base Components (`rounded`: 0.25rem / 4px)**: Form fields, buttons, data chips, segmented tabs, and confidence indicators.
- **Containers (`rounded-lg`: 0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard enterprise cards, data tables, map viewports, and audit panels.
- **Overlay Elements (`rounded-xl`: 0.75rem / 12px)**: Verification modal dialogs and land deed document review drawers.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`)**: Strictly reserved for circular icon-only action triggers and micro status pips (dot indicators).

## Components

### 1. Bilingual Sovereign Gov-Tech Header Bar
- **Structure**: Topmost fixed band (height: 56px) finished in `#0F294A` (Deep Sovereign Navy) with a secondary 3px bottom rule in `#D97706` (Civic Amber).
- **Elements**: Left-aligned State Emblem of India (monochrome white or civic metallic rendering), paired with bilingual typography: "भूमिदृष्टि / BhoomiDrishti" in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Semibold, accompanied by "Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP)".
- **Utilities**: Right-aligned Tehsil/District selector dropdown, bilingual language toggle (`EN` | `हिं`), active session integrity timer, and officer profile with jurisdictional role badge.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign**: Deep Sovereign Navy background (`#0F294A`), pure white text, 4px corner radius, standard height 36px (compact enterprise density). Hover shifts to `#1A365D`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary Action**: White background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#0F294A` text. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tertiary Accent**: Ashoka Blue (`#1D4ED8`) background, white text. Reserved for primary map triggers, vector drawing commits, and GIS overlays.
- **Destructive**: Crisp `#FEF2F2` background, 1px `#FCA5A5` border, `#DC2626` text. Hover shifts to solid `#DC2626` with white text.

### 3. Confidence Score Indicators
Visual meter for AI parcel digitization, automated boundary matching, and OCR deed extraction accuracy:
- **High Confidence (95% - 100%)**: Surface `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `#A7F3D0`. Prefixed with a solid green radial dot and check icon.
- **Medium Confidence (75% - 94%)**: Surface `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `#FDE68A`. Prefixed with an amber warning triangle, triggering manual survey validation.
- **Low Confidence (< 75%)**: Surface `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `#FECACA`. Prefixed with a red flag, routing record directly to the Sub-Divisional Magistrate dispute queue.

### 4. Tabular Data Views & Records Ledger
- **Header Cell**: 32px height, `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` uppercase 11px font (`label-md`), 1px bottom border in `#CBD5E1`.
- **Data Rows**: 40px compact height, alternating subtle zebra striping optional (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`), 1px bottom divider `#E2E8F0`. Hover row: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Data Attributes**: Khasra/Survey numbers rendered in bold `tabular-data` font. Area cells show both metric (hectares) and traditional units (bigha/guntha) in sub-text format.

### 5. Audit Trail & Legal Seal Badges
- **Audit Stamp**: Compact rectangular badge with a 1px border. Contains officer ID, digital timestamp (IST format), and SHA-256 hash snippet. Monospaced tabular numeric styling.
- **Legal Seal**: Displayed on validated RoR (Record of Rights / Jamabandi). Includes the DILRMP digital watermark, certified QR code placeholder, and green "Digitally Signed & Validated" banner.

### 6. Form Inputs & Search Filters
- **Input Fields**: 36px height, pure white background, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, 4px corner radius. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Active focus triggers a 2px solid `#1D4ED8` outer ring with zero ambient glow.
- **Khasra Search Component**: Specialized composite search bar featuring a dual-input dropdown: Tehsil selector segment + instant parcel numeric search with live autocomplete.
