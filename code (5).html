Problems Identified in Legacy Revenue Archives

Centuries-old archival documentation contains catastrophic physical decay, poly-linguistic ambiguities, and cadastral vector distortions that manual entry operators cannot resolve systematically.

blur_on

Severe Media Degradation

Faded carbon ink, water stains, mold colonization, and torn brittle borders dating from British colonial surveys (1880-1940) causing severe pixel bleed.

BhoomiDrishti Mitigation
Deep Super-Resolution GANs (Real-ESRGAN tailored for parchment) paired with adaptive Otsu binarization.
translate

Poly-Script & Dialect Drift

Revenue registers spanning Modi script, Persian/Urdu Nastaliq notations, archaic Hindi variants, Tamil Grantha numerals, and region-specific revenue lexicon.

BhoomiDrishti Mitigation
Domain-fine-tuned IndicBERT v4 & TrOCR with custom regional lexicons and acoustic-phonetic disambiguation.
table_rows_narrow

Inconsistent Formats

Unstandardized hand-drawn table grids, variable column structures between Zamindari settlement ledgers, and post-1950 Khatauni registers.

BhoomiDrishti Mitigation
LayoutLMv3 multi-modal transformer token classification for coordinate-agnostic cell & entity binding.
layers_clear

Cadastral Fraud & Overlaps

Disputed parcel titles, ghost mutations, double allotments across unlinked tehsil sub-registers, and geometric polygon intersections in revenue maps.

BhoomiDrishti Mitigation
PostGIS automated topology validation engine, SHA-256 mutation ledger hashing, and Bhu-Aadhaar verification.
